## The Narrative

The patient onboarding process at POCuro was a significant bottleneck. The medical staff spent 8 hours daily on manual tasks that could be automated. This time was not only costly but also detracted from patient care quality.

## The System

We designed and implemented a comprehensive automation system that included:
- Digital patient intake forms with auto-validation
- Automated document verification with external APIs
- Appointment scheduling integration with their existing calendar system
- Real-time notifications to medical staff
- Data synchronization with their electronic health records

## The Sovereignty

POCuro now owns the entire system. They can modify workflows, add new features, and scale as needed. The system continues to operate even when our team is not directly involved, ensuring continuous improvement and reliability.
