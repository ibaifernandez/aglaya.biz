# ICP & Messaging Reference — AGLAYA Outreach

## ICP Firmographic Criteria

| Variable | Range |
|---|---|
| Employees | 20–200 |
| Annual Revenue | $1M–$50M USD |
| Monthly manual process cost | >$15,000 |
| Industries | Real estate, fintech, e-commerce, SaaS, professional services (legal, health, education) |
| Geography | Spain, Mexico, Colombia, US (Hispanic market) |
| Decision-makers | Founder/CEO, COO, Operations Director, Marketing Director |

## Disqualification Criteria (Do Not Outreach)

- Solo entrepreneurs without a team
- Companies < $1M revenue (typically cannot afford $8K/month)
- Agencies, consultancies (wrong category)
- Companies that have already automated their processes (no pain)
- Decision-maker is not accessible via LinkedIn

## Pain Signal Detection

Look for these signals in LinkedIn profiles, job postings, and company pages:

| Signal | What It Indicates |
|---|---|
| Hiring for "data entry", "operations coordinator", "reporting analyst" | Manual processes not automated |
| Job posting mentioning "Excel", "manual reporting", "spreadsheet management" | Data work that should be automated |
| Team size grew but "systems" or "tools" not mentioned in company description | Scaling without infrastructure |
| Multiple open roles in ops with "fast-paced environment" | Overwhelmed team |
| Company grew 30%+ but no tech/automation roles posted | Growth without systematization |

## LinkedIn DM Templates

### Template A — Process Pain
```
[First name], noticed [Company] has been scaling fast in [industry] — [specific observation, e.g., "three new ops hires in Q1"].

Quick question: how much of your team's time is going into [specific manual process they likely have]?

We map that to a dollar figure for companies your size. Sometimes the number surprises people.
```

### Template B — Job Posting Signal
```
[First name], saw you're hiring a [role from job posting].

Before onboarding someone for [specific responsibility], it might be worth knowing how much of that work is automatable.

We've done this calculation for [similar company type]. The math usually changes the hiring decision.
```

### Template C — Growth Stage
```
[First name], [Company] at [headcount/growth signal] is typically the stage where manual operations start becoming the ceiling.

Not a pitch — just a question: what's the one process your team does daily that you know should be automated but hasn't been?
```

## Cold Email Subject Lines That Work

- "[Company] — the cost of [specific manual process]"
- "One number about [Company]'s ops"
- "Before you hire another [role]"
- "What [similar company] found in their process audit"

## Cold Email Subject Lines to Avoid

- "Quick question" (overused)
- "Following up" (never for first contact)
- "Introduction" (generic)
- Anything with "synergy", "partnership", "collaboration"

## Outreach Sequence Timing

- Message 1: Day 0
- Message 2 follow-up: Day 7 (only if no reply)
- Message 3 with ROI Audit offer: Day 14 (only if still no reply)
- After Day 14 with no reply: remove from sequence, do not contact again for 90 days
