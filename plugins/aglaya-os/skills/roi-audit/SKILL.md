# ROI Audit Skill

Structure an AGLAYA ROI Audit — the $1.500 engagement that qualifies clients and creates the business case for the retainer.

## What the ROI Audit Is

The ROI Audit is not a free diagnosis. It is a two-week paid engagement ($1,500, deductible from the first retainer month) that produces four deliverables:

1. **Process Map** — every manual process the company runs, with estimated monthly cost in time and money
2. **Priority Tree** — what to automate first, and why (impact × feasibility matrix)
3. **System Design** — high-level architecture of the proposed solution
4. **Business Case** — investment vs. projected monthly savings, with explicit assumptions

## The Honesty Clause

If the audit does not identify at least $15,000/month in automatable work, AGLAYA tells the client and does not propose a retainer. This clause is not generosity — it is the mechanism that makes the offer credible.

## Audit Structure

### Phase 1: Process Discovery (Days 1–5)
Interview the client's team leads. Document every recurring manual task:
- Who does it
- How often (daily / weekly / monthly)
- Time per occurrence (minutes/hours)
- Number of people involved
- Tools currently used (or not)
- Error rate and rework cost

**Output:** Raw process inventory spreadsheet

### Phase 2: Cost Quantification (Days 6–8)
For each process:
- Calculate monthly hours consumed: (frequency × time per occurrence × people)
- Apply fully-loaded hourly cost (salary + benefits, typically $25–$75/hour depending on role and region)
- Identify secondary costs: errors, delays, opportunity cost, team morale

**Output:** Process cost matrix with monthly totals

### Phase 3: Priority Tree (Days 9–11)
Score each process on two axes:
- **Automation Feasibility** (1–5): how technically straightforward is automation?
- **Monthly Impact** ($): what does automating this save per month?

Quadrant the matrix: High Impact + High Feasibility → automate first. Low Impact + Low Feasibility → deprioritize or ignore.

**Output:** Priority matrix with top 3–5 processes marked "Build First"

### Phase 4: Business Case (Days 12–14)
Build the ROI document:
- Total current monthly cost of manual processes
- Projected savings from automation (Year 1)
- AGLAYA retainer cost ($8,000/month × 3 months minimum = $24,000)
- Net ROI calculation: (Annual savings - Annual retainer cost) / Annual retainer cost
- Payback period (typically <60 days for well-chosen automations)

**Output:** Business case document + retainer proposal

## Presentation Format

Present findings in this order:
1. The number that matters ("You are spending $X/month on work that should be automatic")
2. The top 3 processes identified, with cost and feasibility score
3. The proposed system design (one paragraph + architecture sketch)
4. The business case: investment, savings, ROI, payback period
5. The next step: retainer proposal or honest "not a fit" conclusion

## Tone

Direct. Numeric. No hedging. The audit speaks in money and time, not in potential and opportunity. The goal is that the client sees the number and the math becomes inevitable.
