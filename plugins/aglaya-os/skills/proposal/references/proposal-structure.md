## Business Case Calculation Example

**Scenario:** E-commerce company, 45 employees, Colombia

Processes identified in ROI Audit:
1. Manual inventory reconciliation — 3 people × 4 hours/day × 22 days = 264 hrs/month × $12/hr = **$3,168/month**
2. Customer support ticket routing — 2 people × 3 hours/day × 22 days = 132 hrs/month × $10/hr = **$1,320/month**
3. Monthly performance reporting — 1 person × 3 days/month × 8 hours = 24 hrs/month × $15/hr = **$360/month**
4. Lead follow-up sequencing — 2 people × 2 hours/day × 22 days = 88 hrs/month × $12/hr = **$1,056/month**
5. Supplier invoice processing — 1 person × 1 hour/day × 22 days = 22 hrs/month × $12/hr = **$264/month**

**Total identified: $6,168/month** ← below threshold, do not propose retainer
