## Existing Cases

### POCURO — Real Estate (Colombia)
**Headline:** 90% Reduction in Unqualified Leads
**Problem:** High inbound lead volume; manual qualification taking 3 days per lead; sales team spending >60% of time on leads that would never convert
**What was built:** Automated lead qualification system scoring 12 criteria in real time; immediate routing of qualified vs. unqualified leads; sales team notified only on high-score leads
**Results:** 90% reduction in unqualified leads reaching sales team; qualification time from 3 days to real-time; sales team capacity effectively tripled without new hires

## One-Liner Format

For use in outreach, proposals, and bios:
"We [result verb] [client type]'s [metric] by [%/number] in [timeframe]."

Examples:
- "We reduced a real estate company's unqualified leads by 90% in 6 weeks."
- "We eliminated 200 manual hours/month from a fintech's reporting cycle."
- "We cut a SaaS company's customer onboarding time from 5 days to 4 hours."
