# 01 — Strategy

## The real end: sell the Stack

The transaction we ultimately want is a **Stack build** — a bespoke automation
system, decenas de miles de dólares. Everything upstream exists to make that
sale honest, desired, and de-risked.

## The value ladder

```
FREE                      PAID (entry)              THE PRODUCT
Interactive diagnostic →  ROI Audit              →  The Stack build
(lead magnet)             (scope-priced session)    (five/six figures)

gives the APPROX number   gives the EXACT number    executes the system
+ brands AGLAYA           + the business case       that stops the bleed
                          that justifies the Stack
```

Each rung is a **taste of the next**:
- The diagnostic gives a *rough* cost of the bleed → creates desire for the exact one.
- The ROI Audit gives the *exact* number + plan → justifies the Stack spend (nobody signs $40K without it).
- The Stack delivers the system.

This is a classic ascension ladder. The diagnostic's job is **top-of-funnel
capture + desire + qualification**, not measurement.

## Positioning unlock: the Stack is a capability, not a catalog

The Stack = **the capacity to assemble a made-to-measure operating system** for
the client's business, from whatever sub-developments their bleed requires.

- **Web · Pulse · Outreach · Kanban Desk** are NOT the menu. They are **receipts**
  — proof the team ships production-grade automation across 4 distinct fronts.
- (Legal Reg·Tech / Consent Ledger is **out** of the Stack — Chile-only, belongs
  to Scanner 21.719's offer.)
- The menu is **"lo que te sangre"**. The answer to almost any operational bleed
  is "that's buildable".

### The dilution tension (must be managed)

"We build anything" risks sounding like a generic dev shop → diluted positioning.
The 4 products give focus + credibility. The balance to strike:

> **Bespoke systems — proven across web, data, sales, and ops.**

Lead with recognizable pains; signal "and whatever else is bleeding you"; anchor
every claim to a receipt. Specific enough to be credible, broad enough to sell.

## The form is a lead magnet, not a form

Not a 3-question HTML `<form>`. A **gamified, Typeform-style** flow (one question
per screen, progress, momentum) whose **payoff is a personalized value asset** —
a "panfleto"/mini business-case that clava la marca a fuego.

Mechanics:
1. Gamified Q&A (diagnose the bleed, not audit the user).
2. **Email-gate** the result → capture (this is the lead magnet; the email is the gold).
3. Deliver a **personalized** deliverable (generic = ignored; tailored = memorable):

   > *"Tu mayor sangría: prospección a mano. Te cuesta ~18 h/sem ≈ $X/mes.
   > Esto lo mata AGLAYA Outreach — ya lo montamos para Bill Capital.
   > Así ensamblaríamos tu sistema: [piezas]. ¿La cifra exacta? El ROI Audit."*

4. Soft CTA → the ROI Audit (next rung).

## What each question must do

Working backward from the sale, every question either **sizes the prize**
(builds desire) or **gates the fit** (can this become a Stack deal). The three
real gates, reframed from current-state metrics into felt pain:

| Gate | Old (robotic) | New (felt) | Predicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain size | "% manual?" | "What bleeds you most?" + "Do you already have a tool for it?" | Is the problem worth a custom build? |
| Fundability | "Monthly budget?" | (kept, plain) | Can they pay five figures? |
| Foundation | "Data infrastructure?" | "Where does your data live?" | Can we build on it? |

The honest "not yet" stays — but now it protects the **big sale**, not a form.
