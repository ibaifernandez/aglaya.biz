## 0. The one sticky line (Commander's Intent)

> **"Your manual work is quietly bleeding ≈ $X a year — and the patchwork holding
> it together breaks the moment you get busy."**

Everything serves that line. Simple · concrete · unexpected (the size) · credible
(cited) · emotional (the drain) · story (your Friday back).

## 1. The Job (JTBD)

> *When my business grows and the busywork multiplies, I want the repetitive work
> to run itself reliably, so I can get back to growing instead of babysitting it —
> and stop the energy drain.*

- **Functional:** get the hours back, stop the manual work.
- **Emotional:** stop the drain, get my head back, peace of mind.
- **Social:** look like a real operation, not a duct-tape mess.
- **Forces:** amplify Push (the bleed) + Pull (a system that runs itself); KILL
  Anxiety (risk reversal, proof, "you don't lift a finger") + Habit (we handle migration).
- **Real competitor:** not other agencies — **doing nothing** + **the Zapier
  Frankenstein they half-built and now babysit.** Position against that.

## 2. Value ladder = AIDA

```
Scorecard quiz  →  Personalized report  →  ROI Audit (paid)  →  The Stack (build)
   ATTENTION         INTEREST + DESIRE        DESIRE→CONVICTION      ACTION
   (free, hook)      (the number, the bleed)  (exact number + plan)  (5-6 figures)
```
Risk reversal across the ladder: **the ROI Audit fee credits 100% against the
Stack build** ($5K audit → $50K build → pay $45K). The audit manufactures the
belief that justifies the build.

## 3. Landing (scorecard hook + 3 Cs + brand voice)

- **Hook (concept = a score):** *"What's your manual work actually costing you?"*
  (moving-toward + pain; frames a number they'll want to know).
- **3 Cs:** Clarity (what they get: their bleed number + the fix), Credibility
  (built 4×: DJ Amapola, Elm St, Bill Capital, Massiva; cited benchmarks),
  Connection (their language: "falls through the cracks", "chasing invoices").
- **Friction-killers:** "6 questions. 2 minutes. A number you can act on."
- **CTA:** *"See what it's costing me →"* (action + outcome, not "Start").
- Voice: declarative, no fluff adjectives, data-led.

## 4. The quiz (form-cro + mom-test + scorecard)

**Structure:** one question per screen · progress bar · branching (fork → depth →
gates) · ~6 answered · save progress · number keypad on counts · single column.

**Mom-Test rule:** every question asks a COUNTABLE FACT (hours, people, tools,
spend), never a self-rating ("how automated are you 1–10" = vanity, banned — that
was the old slider's sin). Facts feed the costing model AND can't be flattered.

**Q1 — THE FORK (commitment + routing):** *"Which one is bleeding you most right now?"*
(pick 1; optional 2nd, skippable)
- Reports built by hand every month
- Chasing leads / a CRM nobody updates
- Chasing invoices / getting paid late
- Copy-pasting data between tools
- Drowning in repetitive admin
- Data scattered across spreadsheets

**→ EMAIL GATE here** (after the hook commits them; teaser of result unlocks on
submit). *Decision flagged §8.* Email only; name optional; company inferred from domain.

**Q2 — depth:** *"How many people touch this every week?"* (1 · 2–3 · 4–10 · 10+)
**Q3 — depth:** *"Across them, roughly how many hours a week?"* (<5 · 5–15 · 15–40 · 40+)
**Q4 — depth:** *"What runs it today?"* (Nothing/by hand · Spreadsheets · An off-the-shelf tool · A patchwork I babysit — Zapier/Make)
**Q5 — gate (size):** *"How big is your team?"* (Just me · 2–10 · 11–50 · 50+)
**Q6 — gate (fundability, factual not hypothetical):** *"Roughly what do you pay monthly for tools in this area?"* (<$100 · $100–500 · $500–2K · $2K+)

Q3 × Q2 × loaded rate (doc 08) = the labor bleed. Q6 = SaaS bleed + fundability
signal. Q4 = the wedge ("patchwork I babysit" → the Frankenstein angle). Q5 = tier.

## 5. Scoring (scorecard tiers × costing model)

- **The number** = status-quo bleed range from doc 08 (labor + SaaS, transparent
  assumption ranges). The headline.
- **Tiers** (fit for a Stack build):
  - **Qualified** — big bleed + fundable + foundation → "strong fit" (the sweet spot).
  - **Borderline** — real bleed, one gate soft → "could be a fit".
  - **Not yet** — too small / unfundable / nothing to build on → honest "not yet".
- Tier drives the report's CTA (scorecard: different CTA per tier).

## 6. The report (made-to-stick + offer + influence)

1. **Headline number:** *"Your manual work is bleeding ≈ $X/year."* (range)
2. **Sticky line:** + *"…and your patchwork breaks when you're busiest."*
3. **Breakdown (concrete, cited):** your bleed = N people × H hrs/wk × loaded rate
   + your tool stack. Transparent assumptions shown. (credibility = BLS/Atradius cited)
4. **Emotional payload (Mother Teresa, about THEM):** *"That's not just money. It's
   the Friday you never get back and the head-space you've lost."*
5. **Prescription (the wedge):** *"This is buildable. We've built it 4 times —*
   *[receipt]. We'd assemble yours."* (authority + social proof + admit-weakness honesty)
6. **Tier CTA:** qualified → *"Book your ROI Audit"*; borderline → *"Let's talk"*;
   not-yet → honest *"Here's what to fix first"* + nurture (reciprocity: real help).
7. **Risk reversal (named guarantee):** *"The Honesty Clause: if the audit doesn't
   find at least $X in automatable waste, you don't pay."*
8. **Scarcity (real):** *"We take a limited number of audits each month."*

PDF = personalized, shareable (lands in their next ops meeting).

## 7. The offer (hundred-million)

- **Value equation:** Dream (stop the bleed, get head back) × Likelihood (built 4×,
  cited) ÷ Time (2-wk audit) ÷ Effort (done-for-you, they don't lift a finger).
- **Anchor price to the cost of NOT solving** (= their bleed number) — not to competitors.
- **Risk reversal:** audit fee credited 100% to build + the Honesty Clause guarantee.
- **Bonuses (stack, named, real $):** e.g. the diagnostic report itself; a recorded
  teardown; a prioritized automation roadmap.
- **Scarcity:** real monthly audit capacity (small studio).
- **MAGIC names (test):**
  - Diagnostic: **"The Busywork Bleed Scorecard"** / "What's Your Manual Work Costing You?"
  - Audit: **"The 2-Week ROI Audit"** (credited to your build).
  - Guarantee: **"The Honesty Clause."**
