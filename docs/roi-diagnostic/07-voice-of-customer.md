## Buy-vs-hire — the read

Done-for-you **is supported** by real discourse ("pay someone to handle it all").
BUT not unanimous — a real segment reaches for Zapier/Make first. **The wedge:
lead with operate/maintain/don't-break, NOT build.** The true competitor isn't
other agencies — it's the **Zapier-glued Frankenstein the owner half-built and now
babysits**. Sell against *fragility + mental overhead*, not against software.
Angle: *"the tool is the easy part; keeping it running when you're slammed is the
job you don't have time for."*

## Pains we were UNDER-weighting (surfaced unprompted)

1. **Fragility — "breaks when busy".** Deeper than "slow": the patchwork *fails at
   the worst moment*. Foreground this; bigger than "save time".
2. **Energy drain, not just hours.** Owners feel *depleted/distracted*, not merely
   time-poor. "Get your head back" beats "save X hours".
3. **Empty/un-updated CRM specifically.** Not lack of a tool — the manual labor the
   tool still demands. Angle: "we make your existing CRM actually work" → dodges
   the "not another tool" objection.
4. **Collections as margin-killer**, not annoyance ("chasing invoices wiped the
   profit"). Finance automation = margin recovery.
